## ENGAGEMENT STRATEGY

**Daily:**
- Like + comment on 3-5 posts in your feed (other marketers, prospects)
- Respond to comments on your posts within 2 hours
- Keep replies conversational (not salesy)

**Weekly:**
- React to posts from people in your target audience
- Share insights on other's posts (add value, not self-promotion)
- Monitor DMs and respond to booking requests same day

**Monthly:**
- Identify 5-10 warm leads (commenters, engagers) → DM with non-salesy message
- Analyze post performance (which topics resonate?)
- Adjust next month's calendar based on data
