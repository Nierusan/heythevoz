## MONITORING & OPTIMIZATION

### Daily (Mon-Fri)
- Check spend pacing vs budget
- Monitor CTR (target: >1.5%)
- Pause keywords with <0.5% CTR (low relevance)

### Every 3 Days
- Review conversion metrics
- Check cost per conversion (target: CHF 10-20)
- Adjust max CPC based on performance

### Weekly (Friday)
- Summarize performance (spend, clicks, conversions, CPA)
- Identify winners (keywords >2% CTR, CPA <CHF 15)
- Double budget on winners in Week 2
- Pause underperformers (CPA >CHF 25)

### Week 4 Decision
- Which campaign drives best leads?
- Which keywords are profitable?
- Should we 2x the budget on winners?
- Should we pause losers?

## TROUBLESHOOTING

**If CTR is low (<1%):**
- Ad copy doesn't match search intent
- Keywords too broad
- Max CPC too low (losing impressions)
→ Refine keywords, test new ad copy, increase CPC slightly

**If CPA is high (>CHF 25):**
- Landing page conversion rate low
- Wrong audience (wrong keywords)
- Bid too aggressive on low-value keywords
→ Test landing page variants, pause low-CTR keywords, lower bids on tail keywords

**If no conversions:**
- Conversion tracking broken (check pixel)
- Landing page not loading correctly
- Budget too low to accumulate data (2-week minimum)
→ Verify tracking, test landing page, wait for data
